# Lote 03 — S03 Residential burglary

## C-S03-01
Calibração: As digitais do réu na janela arrombada podem vir da vistoria do seguro; mas estão na face interna do caixilho, e o laudo assinado pelo réu registra só inspeção externa; o réu diz que abriu a janela para checar a vedação.

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 32, an insurance appraiser, is charged with residential burglary of a detached house. While the owners were away for a weekend, someone forced open a rear window and took jewelry, a laptop, and about $800 in cash.

Investigators lifted two fingerprints from the inside surface of the forced window frame, the side facing the room. Both matched {NAME}. Fresh pry marks on the outside of the frame matched a flat screwdriver, a common tool. About five weeks earlier, {NAME} had inspected storm damage to the rear windows for the owners' insurance company. The inspection report, signed by {NAME}, describes an "exterior inspection only."

The owners testified that they did not let anyone into the house during the inspection and did not see the inspector open any window. A fingerprint expert testified that prints on a painted frame can last for weeks and that their age cannot be determined.

{NAME} testified that {he} opened the window from outside to check the seal, which would have meant touching the inner surface, and that {he} did not record it because it was routine. {His} roommate confirmed that {NAME} was home Saturday evening but away Sunday afternoon, when the neighbors believe the burglary happened.

## C-S03-02

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 29, a restaurant shift manager, is charged with residential burglary of an apartment from which a gold watch, two rings, and a tablet were taken between 1:00 p.m. and 4:00 p.m.

Two days after the burglary, {NAME} sold the gold watch to a pawn shop, showing {his} own driver's license and signing the receipt. The watch had a unique engraving that the owner described before it was recovered. Its market value is about $1,200. The other items were never found.

{NAME} testified that {he} bought the watch for $150 from a stranger at a gas station and sold it to the pawn shop for $400 the same week. {He} could not give the stranger's name or a clear description. The gas station's cameras keep footage for only 24 hours.

## C-S03-03
Calibração: A pegada é de um tênis muito vendido e o celular do réu estava na área, onde mora um amigo, cujo álibi tem uma lacuna; nenhum objeto furtado foi encontrado.

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 25, a junior accountant, is charged with residential burglary of a townhouse. Someone kicked in the back door on a weekday morning and took two laptops and a gaming console. The owners were at work from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. that day.

Investigators photographed a muddy shoe print on the kitchen floor. The print matches the sole pattern and size of a running shoe found in {NAME}'s closet. A shoe expert testified that the model is one of the best-selling running shoes in the country.

Cell tower data place {NAME}'s phone in the area of the townhouse between 9:00 and 11:30 a.m. {NAME}'s friend lives about four blocks away and testified that {NAME} came over that morning to help move furniture, but left "for a while" at some point; the friend could not say when or for how long. None of the stolen items were found.

## C-S03-04

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 38, a sales representative, is charged with residential burglary of a house. Someone broke a glass panel in the back door and stole a coin collection worth about $9,000.

A cigarette butt found on the back patio, about two meters from the broken door, contained {NAME}'s DNA. {NAME} lived in the house next door until three months before the burglary and sometimes smoked on the homeowners' patio during backyard gatherings. A lab analyst testified that the DNA showed little degradation, "more consistent with days than with months" outdoors, but that the age of a sample cannot be determined with certainty. It had rained twice in the week before the burglary.

The homeowners had shown the coin collection to several visitors over the years, including {NAME}. A neighbor's camera recorded a car similar to {NAME}'s, a common silver sedan, passing on the street 40 minutes before the estimated time of the burglary. The plate is not readable.

{NAME} testified that {he} was at {his} new apartment that evening. {He} has no witnesses. Bank records show that {NAME} had fallen behind on rent and had received an eviction warning the week before.

## C-S03-05

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 27, a graphic designer, is charged with residential burglary of an apartment {he} used to share with the complainant. Three months after {NAME} moved out, someone entered the apartment while it was empty and took a camera, two lenses, and an envelope with $1,500 in cash.

There was no forced entry. {NAME} admits never returning {his} key. The complainant testified that two other people also had keys: a dog walker and a former partner of the complainant. Both testified that they did not enter the apartment that day.

Two days after the burglary, {NAME} deposited $1,400 in cash into {his} bank account.

{NAME} testified that the message was about filing a small claims case and that the cash came from selling furniture to buyers {he} found online; {he} produced one listing for a sofa priced at $300. {NAME} says {he} was working alone at home that afternoon.

## C-S03-06
Calibração: O réu foi achado de madrugada dentro da garagem anexa, com luvas e lanterna, e diz que procurava um gato perdido (há post às 1:50); mas o morador viu a pessoa mexendo no trinco por um minuto e há arranhões de aparência recente no trinco, que não se sabe se são antigos.

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 34, a hospital pharmacist who works night shifts, is charged with residential burglary after being found at about 3:30 a.m. inside the attached garage of a house two blocks from {his} own home. Under state law, an attached garage is part of the dwelling.

The homeowner heard noises, looked out, and saw a person with a flashlight working at the side door of the garage for about a minute before stepping inside. The homeowner called 911. Police arrived within six minutes and found {NAME} inside the garage, wearing gloves and holding a flashlight, next to a shelf with power tools. Nothing was missing.

{NAME} testified that {his} cat had escaped that evening and that {he} was searching nearby yards. {He} said {he} heard a noise inside the garage, found the side door unlocked, and stepped in to look for the cat. A "lost cat" post from {NAME} appeared on a local community app at 1:50 a.m. The cat was found two days later, three streets away.

The homeowner testified that the side door was locked earlier that night. Officers photographed fresh-looking scratches around the latch; a detective said they were consistent with a thin tool. No tool was found on {NAME}, and no older photos of the door exist for comparison.

## C-S03-07
Calibração: O notebook furtado estava na sala comum da casa do réu, e um inquilino sumiu; mas o histórico do notebook mostra o e-mail pessoal do réu aberto nele no dia seguinte ao furto, e o réu tinha uma janela de 50 min no almoço.

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 26, a barista, is charged with residential burglary of a house from which a laptop, a tablet, and a set of headphones were stolen during the day.

The laptop had tracking software. Two days after the burglary, the owner located it at a house where {NAME} rents a room with three other tenants. Police found the laptop in a shared living room. Its browser history shows {NAME}'s personal email account being opened on it the day after the burglary. The tablet and headphones were not found.

One of the other tenants had moved out the day after the burglary and could not be located. {NAME} testified that this tenant brought the laptop home, said it had been "bought cheap from a friend," and let {NAME} use it once to check email. The remaining two tenants testified that they had never seen the laptop but rarely used the living room.

A neighbor of the burglarized house saw a person on a bicycle leave the house carrying a backpack. {NAME} owns a bicycle, as do the other tenants. Phone location data place {NAME} at work during most of the estimated burglary window, but there is a 50-minute gap during {his} lunch break; the house is about 10 minutes away by bicycle.

## C-S03-08
Calibração: Um vizinho viu o carteiro indo para os fundos da casa às 2:00, mas o réu ficou só 4 min no endereço; o GPS mostra que o veículo postal voltou à rua às 4:40, depois do fim da rota, e ficou parado 12 min; ninguém viu a entrada.

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 31, a postal mail carrier, is charged with residential burglary of a house on {his} delivery route. Someone entered through an unlocked back door during the day and took a tablet, a laptop, and several pieces of jewelry.

A neighbor testified to seeing {NAME}, whom the neighbor recognized as the regular mail carrier, walking along the side of the house toward the backyard at about 2:00 p.m. Postal scan records show that {NAME} delivered a package to that house at 1:57 p.m., leaving it at the front door. The postal vehicle's GPS shows {him} at the next stop four minutes later.

The GPS also shows that, at 4:40 p.m., after {NAME}'s route had ended, the postal vehicle returned to the same street and stayed parked two houses away for 12 minutes. The homeowners left for work at 8:00 a.m. and returned at 6:30 p.m. A different neighbor saw a person in a gray sweatshirt in the backyard "sometime in the afternoon" but could not describe the face.

{NAME} testified that {he} walked toward the side gate at 2:00 p.m. only to look for a safer place to leave the package, and that {he} returned at 4:40 p.m. to eat a late lunch in a shaded spot {he} often uses.

## C-S03-09
Calibração: Vidro e fibras são provas de classe, e o réu tem explicação parcial para o vidro; o réu foi parado perto do local pouco depois do arrombamento.

The defendant, {NAME} {SURNAME}, 36, a freelance photographer, is charged with residential burglary of a house where a glass patio door was smashed and electronics worth about $4,000 were taken.

Police stopped {NAME} about one kilometer from the house, 50 minutes after a neighbor reported the sound of breaking glass. A lab found three tiny glass fragments on {NAME}'s jacket with the same refractive index as the patio door glass, a type used in millions of doors. Blue fibers on the broken door frame were consistent with {NAME}'s jacket, a mass-produced item.

{NAME} testified that {he} spent the evening at a friend's home helping repair a broken window, which explains the glass. The friend confirmed this and showed a receipt for window glass bought that afternoon, but an expert testified that the friend's window used a different type of glass. No stolen items were found on {NAME} or in {his} car. {NAME} has a conviction for burglary from 11 years ago.

## C-S03-10

A person who shared a jail cell with {NAME} for three days, after {his} arrest on an unrelated traffic warrant, testified that {NAME} described "taking a safe out of a house on the lake road" and said it took two people to carry it. In exchange for testifying, the informant received a reduced sentence on a fraud charge. Those details had appeared in a local news article.

Investigators found that {NAME} paid off a $6,000 car loan in cash two weeks after the burglary. {NAME} testified that the money came from selling a boat and produced a handwritten bill of sale; the buyer could not be located. {NAME} denies ever talking about the burglary.
